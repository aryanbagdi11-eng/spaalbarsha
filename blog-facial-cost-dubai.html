Pricing

How Much Does a
Facial Cost in Dubai?

Facial pricing in Dubai varies more than people expect, mostly depending on what's actually included rather than just the spa's location.

What affects the price

Three things matter most: how many steps are included (a basic cleanse-and-mask differs a lot from a multi-step treatment), the products used, and the length of the session. A 30-minute express facial and a 90-minute deep treatment aren't priced the same, even at the same spa.

What's usually included at a fair price

Cleansing, exfoliation, mask application, and basic hydration should be standard at most price points. Extras like LED therapy, specialised serums, or extended massage time typically push the price up.

Is a more expensive facial always better?

Not necessarily — for a basic skincare reset, a simpler, shorter facial often does the job. Higher prices tend to reflect either more advanced equipment or a longer session, not automatically better results for every skin type.

Getting an accurate price

We don't publish one fixed rate, since it depends on the specific treatment. When you book, the therapist confirms the exact price with you directly before your appointment — no surprises at the end.
