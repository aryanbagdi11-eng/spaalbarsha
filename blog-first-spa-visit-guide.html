First-Timers

What to Expect
From Your First Visit.

First time booking with us? Here's exactly what happens, from your first message to leaving your appointment.

Booking

Message us on WhatsApp or fill in the form on our homepage with your preferred treatment and timing. We'll confirm your slot and send you the exact location.

Arriving

Come a few minutes early to settle in. There's no lengthy check-in process — just let us know you've arrived and we'll get you started close to your booked time.

During your treatment

Your therapist will check your preferences at the start — pressure for a massage, or specific concerns for a facial. Speak up at any point if something needs adjusting.

Payment

Payment is handled directly at the end of your session, by cash or card depending on what's available that day — we don't take payment upfront when you book.

Booking again

If a particular therapist worked well for you, mention it next time you book and we'll try to place you with them again, depending on availability.
