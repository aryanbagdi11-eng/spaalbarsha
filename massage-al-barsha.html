Massage in Al Barsha.

Swedish, deep tissue and sports massage from licensed therapists.

What's included

A choice of Swedish, deep tissue or sports technique, delivered by a therapist trained specifically in that style. All oils and equipment are provided.

Typical duration

Most sessions run 60 or 90 minutes. Mention your preference when booking — some slots also accommodate a shorter 45-minute express session.

Who it's for

Swedish suits general relaxation, deep tissue suits chronic tension from desk work or travel, and sports massage suits pre- or post-workout recovery — tell us which fits and we'll match the right therapist.

Common Questions

Booking Massage.

Can I choose the massage type?

Yes — Swedish, deep tissue or sports, mentioned when you book.

Is this suitable for first-time guests?

Yes, Swedish massage in particular is a common starting point if you're not sure what you need.
