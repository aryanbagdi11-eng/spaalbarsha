Common Questions

Before you
get matched.

Is Spa Al Barsha an actual spa?

Not exactly — think of us as a local booking concierge. We connect guests to vetted, independently-run spas across Al Barsha 1 and Al Barsha 2, and the treatment itself takes place at their location.

Does it cost anything to use?

Nothing. The spa's usual treatment price is all you pay when you arrive; connecting you with them doesn't cost anything extra.

What's the difference between Al Barsha 1 and Al Barsha 2?

They're neighbouring sub-communities within Al Barsha. We cover spas in both, so mention which one you're closer to when you request a match.

How fast will I hear back?

During daytime hours, replies from a matched spa typically arrive in well under 15 minutes.
